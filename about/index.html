About HouseTech Group

Your trusted partner in professional kitchen appliance manufacturing

Company Overview

HouseTech Group is a leading manufacturer specializing in the research, development, and production of high-quality kitchen appliances. With over 15 years of industry experience, we have established ourselves as a reliable B2B partner for businesses across more than 50 countries worldwide.

Our product portfolio includes Range Hoods (Kitchen Hoods, Cooker Hoods, Extractor Hoods, Chimney Hoods), Gas Stoves & Hobs (Built-in Gas/Electric/Ceramic/Induction Hobs), Built-in Ovens (Electric/Gas/Convection/Steam Ovens), Air Fryers, and Gas Water Heaters — covering all essential kitchen appliance categories.

Our Mission

To provide innovative, high-quality kitchen appliance solutions at competitive factory-direct prices while delivering exceptional OEM/ODM services to our global partners.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted kitchen appliance manufacturer, recognized for innovation, quality, and customer-centric service.

Core Values

  • Quality First: Every product meets international standards with rigorous quality control processes.
  • Innovation: Continuous R&D investment to develop cutting-edge products that meet evolving market demands.
  • Integrity: Transparent business practices and honest communication with all stakeholders.
  • Customer Focus: Understanding and exceeding customer expectations through personalized solutions.
  • Sustainability: Committed to environmentally friendly manufacturing practices and energy-efficient products.

Key Facts

  • Established: Over 15 years in kitchen appliance manufacturing
  • Factory Size: 30,000+ square meters of modern production facility
  • Employees: 500+ skilled workers and engineers
  • Production Capacity: 1 million units per year
  • Export Markets: 50+ countries across Europe, Middle East, Southeast Asia, Africa, and Americas
  • Certifications: ISO9001, CE, CB, ETL, SASO, GCC
  • R&D Team: 50+ engineers dedicated to product innovation

Milestones

  • 2010 — Company founded with focus on range hood manufacturing
  • 2013 — Expanded product line to gas stoves/hobs
  • 5 — Added oven production line
  • 2017 — Achieved ISO9001 certification; entered Middle East market
  • 2019 — Launched air fryer product series; R&D center established
  • 2021 — Factory expansion to 30,000 sqm; ETL certification obtained
  • 2023 — Water heater line launched; export to 50+ countries milestone
  • 2024 — Digital transformation; smart appliance R&D initiated
