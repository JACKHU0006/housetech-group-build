Company Overview

HouseTech Group is a leading B2B manufacturer specializing in the research, development, and production of high-quality kitchen appliances. Established in 2010, HouseTech has grown from a specialized range hood factory into a comprehensive kitchen appliance manufacturer serving global markets.

With over 15 years of industry experience, HouseTech Group has established itself as a reliable B2B partner for businesses across more than 50 countries worldwide. Our product portfolio includes Range Hoods (Kitchen Hoods, Cooker Hoods, Extractor Hoods, Chimney Hoods, Commercial Hoods), Gas Stoves & Built-in Hobs (Gas/Electric/Ceramic/Induction Hobs), Built-in Ovens (Electric/Gas/Convection/Steam Ovens), Air Fryers, Induction Cookers, Ceramic Cookers, and Gas Water Heaters — covering all essential kitchen appliance categories for residential and commercial applications.

Our Mission

HouseTech Group's mission is to provide innovative, high-quality kitchen appliance solutions at competitive factory-direct prices while delivering exceptional OEM/ODM services to our global partners. We aim to be the most trusted manufacturing backbone for kitchen appliance brands worldwide.

Our Vision

HouseTech Group's vision is to become the world's most trusted kitchen appliance manufacturer, recognized for innovation, quality, and customer-centric service. We envision a future where every kitchen, in every home and commercial space globally, benefits from HouseTech's reliable and efficient appliances.
