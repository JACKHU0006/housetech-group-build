Why Work at HouseTech Group?

Join a dynamic team at the forefront of kitchen appliance innovation. HouseTech Group offers competitive compensation, professional development opportunities, and a collaborative work environment where your contributions make a real difference across 50+ global markets.

Competitive Salary

Above-market compensation packages based on experience and skills, with annual performance reviews and salary adjustments.

Growth Opportunities

Clear career paths and promotion opportunities within the HouseTech organization. Many team leaders started from entry-level positions.

Training & Development

Continuous learning programs, skill enhancement opportunities, and professional certification support for all HouseTech employees.

Global Exposure

Work with international clients and participate in global trade shows. HouseTech team members regularly attend Canton Fair, IFA, and other exhibitions worldwide.
