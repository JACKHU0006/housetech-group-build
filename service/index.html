HouseTech Group Service Portfolio

From product design to global delivery, HouseTech Group provides comprehensive services tailored for international kitchen appliance brands.

Core Service

OEM / ODM Customization

HouseTech Group offers comprehensive OEM and ODM services for kitchen appliance brands worldwide. Customize your products with precision to match your market requirements.

  • Custom Logo & Branding: Your brand identity on HouseTech quality products
  • Color & Finish Options: Wide range of colors, materials, and surface treatments
  • Specification Adjustments: Modify dimensions, power ratings, voltage, and features
  • Packaging Design: Retail packaging, bulk packaging, and gift box options
Quality

Quality Assurance

HouseTech Group's rigorous ISO9001-certified quality system guarantees every product meets the highest international standards through our 5-stage QC process.

  • ISO9001 Certified Processes: Systematic quality management across all HouseTech production
  • Multi-Stage Testing: Incoming, in-process, and final product inspections
  • Function & Safety Testing: Every unit tested for performance and safety compliance
  • Certification Support: CE, CB, ETL, SASO, GCC and more for target markets
Logistics

Global Logistics

HouseTech Group handles worldwide shipping with flexible terms and comprehensive logistics support for kitchen appliance deliveries to any destination.

  • DDP (Delivered Duty Paid): HouseTech arranges door-to-door delivery including customs clearance
  • FOB / CIF / EXW: Multiple shipping terms available per your preference
  • Multi-Port Options: Ship from major Chinese ports with consolidation services
  • Documentation Support: Commercial invoice, packing list, CO, BL handling
Support

After-Sales Support

HouseTech Group's commitment extends well beyond the point of delivery. We provide comprehensive after-sales services to protect your business and end customers.

  • 2-Year Warranty: Standard warranty with optional extension available
  • Spare Parts Supply: Guaranteed spare parts availability for 3+ years
  • Technical Documentation: Manuals, installation guides, and troubleshooting docs
  • Remote Assistance: Online diagnosis and support from HouseTech technicians
Certifications

Custom Certification Support

HouseTech Group assists international partners in obtaining all necessary certifications for their target markets, ensuring full regulatory compliance.

  • CE (Europe): Full CE compliance testing and documentation for HouseTech products
  • ETL / UL (North America): ETL listed and UL recognized components for US/Canada markets
  • SASO / GCC (Middle East): SASO certification and GCC conformity for Saudi Arabia and Gulf states
  • CB Scheme: International CB certification facilitating multi-country approvals
  • Additional Markets: INMETRO (Brazil), PSE (Japan), and more available on request
Brand Solutions

Private Label / White Label

HouseTech Group offers private label and white label solutions for businesses looking to launch their own kitchen appliance brand without manufacturing complexity.

  • White Label: HouseTech existing designs with your brand — fastest time to market
  • Private Label: Fully customized products built exclusively for your brand identity
  • MOQ Flexibility: Starting from 100 units for standard white label products
  • Full-Service Package: From design to delivery, HouseTech handles everything
