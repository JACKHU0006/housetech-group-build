OEM / ODM Manufacturing

OEM Services: Produce products under your brand with your specifications. We handle manufacturing while you focus on marketing and distribution.

ODM Services: Choose from our existing designs and customize them to fit your market needs — including appearance, features, and packaging.

  • Custom logo and branding
  • Color and finish options
  • Packaging design (retail & bulk)
  • Product specification adjustments
  • Certification support for target markets

Product Customization

We offer flexible customization to meet diverse market requirements:

  • Size and dimension modifications
  • Control panel design (mechanical, touch, digital)
  • Voltage adaptation (110V, 220V, 240V)
  • Gas type compatibility (LPG, NG, etc.)
  • Language localization on controls and manuals
  • Safety feature additions per regional requirements
